What this result does not mean

TraceCheck cannot see arbitrary browsing history, passwords, other sites' localStorage, or a tracker company's server-side profile. The scores describe observable signals and tests, not overall computer security.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

We fingerprint you to explain fingerprinting — not to track you.

01

Local by default

Canvas, WebGL, audio, fonts, client hints and storage tests are calculated in your browser.

02

No hidden profile

The core scan has no account or server-side fingerprint history. Previous-scan comparison is stored locally.

03

Explicit external tests

IP intelligence, public-STUN and tracker-request tests only run after you click their buttons and explain what leaves the browser.
