What this result does not mean

TraceCheck cannot determine whether your device has malware, whether an account has been compromised, or whether a specific fraud system distrusts you. The scores describe observable browser/network signals, not overall computer security.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

We fingerprint you to explain fingerprinting — not to track you.

01

Local calculation

Canvas, WebGL and browser attributes are combined and hashed in your browser.

02

No fingerprint account

The MVP has no database, login, analytics identifier or server-side fingerprint history.

03

Minimal network endpoint

The API returns a small allow-list of connection metadata already visible to the site.
